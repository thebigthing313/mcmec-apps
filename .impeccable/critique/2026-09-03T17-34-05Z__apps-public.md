## Overall Impression

Content written by someone who understands the statutory obligation; an interface that does not yet understand the resident. Organized as a filing cabinet — by document type, in agency vocabulary, reverse-chronological — when its job is to answer one urgent question fast. Biggest opportunity: one wide operational band on the home page answering "is anything scheduled near me tonight," and the discipline to let the other five doors get quieter.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Spray Schedule answers the primary question wrongly

routes/mosquito-control/spray-schedule.tsx:56 sorts mission_date DESCENDING with no upcoming/past split; status badge comes from a stored field, not the date. Live on staging the top card is a past date badged "Scheduled". Same bug fixed for Meetings this morning in e6877ea (shared meeting-periods module, Upcoming default, shared empty-state copy); the more urgent page got none of it.

CORRECTION (from the user, after the run): a past-dated mission still badged "Scheduled" is EXPECTED. Status is advanced by hand by staff, so there is a real lag window before a mission is marked completed. Do NOT derive the badge from mission_date vs now — that would turn a lifecycle field into a computed one, which ADR 0001 forbids. Spray ops are weather-dependent, so a past mission genuinely may not have happened.

SETTLED FIX (decided with the user): keep `status` authored and untouched — badges unchanged for now. Split into **Upcoming** (ascending, soonest first) and **Past** (descending, below/collapsed). Nothing else.

Explicitly REJECTED: a "Tonight" group. Missions run overnight — 3AM–8AM as readily as 7PM–midnight — so a 3AM mission dated Sept 4 is colloquially the night of Sept 3. "Tonight" would be wrong for about half of all missions, and wrong in the dangerous direction (a resident concluding they are clear when they are not). Upcoming/Past is unambiguous because it is purely relative to now. Do not reintroduce "Tonight" in any later pass.

Also rejected for now: "outcome not yet confirmed" phrasing on Past. Badges stay as authored.

Boundary detail: compute the Upcoming/Past split from the END of the mission (mission_date + end_time), not from mission_date alone, so a mission in progress stays in Upcoming while trucks are still out. Where end_time < start_time the mission crosses midnight and ends on the following day — account for that or an overnight mission drops into Past mid-run.

Empty state: "No upcoming spray missions scheduled" (not "tonight"). This is the reassurance state the site currently lacks entirely.
