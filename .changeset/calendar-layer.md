The shared Calendar layer the Portal's Calendar, Upcoming card and Holiday Schedule are built on: one `CalendarItem` shape, a mapper per source (Staff Events, Holidays, Meetings in Edison's time, Spray Missions fanned out to their rain dates), Calendar ordering, week lane-packing, the month / upcoming / Holidays-in-a-year filters, the labels each surface reads them with, and `useCalendarItems`.
